About this ebook

Screens are woven into nearly everything we do, which makes them both useful and quietly exhausting. Constant notifications, infinite feeds, and the habit of reaching for a phone in every spare moment can leave attention frayed and rest hard to find. This book offers a calm, realistic way to step back without abandoning technology altogether.

A digital detox here does not mean throwing your devices in a drawer forever. It means becoming honest about how you use them, trimming what drains you, and rebuilding healthier patterns on your own terms. Each chapter gives practical steps you can adapt to your life, so the changes actually last beyond the first hopeful week.

What's inside

  • An honest look at where your screen time really goes.
  • Practical ways to tame constant notifications.
  • Simple boundaries that protect focus and rest.
  • Screen free routines for mornings and evenings.
  • Tactics for breaking the mindless scroll habit.
  • Ideas for filling freed up time meaningfully.
  • A flexible plan you can actually keep.
